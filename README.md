# Identification of Genetically Regulated Metabolites for Risk Stratification in Early-Onset Colorectal Cancer

**MSc Genomic Medicine Research Project | Imperial College London**

## Overview

Early-onset colorectal cancer (EOCRC), generally defined as colorectal cancer diagnosed before the age of 50, represents an important public health challenge. Understanding the genetic and metabolic factors associated with colorectal cancer may contribute to improved risk stratification and the identification of potential biomarkers.

This project investigates genetic associations with circulating metabolites using metabolite genome-wide association study (mGWAS) summary statistics. The analysis focuses on identifying statistically significant SNP–metabolite associations, prioritising genetic signals, and exploring their potential biological relevance to colorectal cancer.

## Research Aim

To identify and characterise genetically regulated metabolic traits that may be relevant to risk stratification in early-onset colorectal cancer.

### Objectives

1. Perform quality control and standardisation of mGWAS summary statistics.
2. Identify genome-wide significant SNP–metabolite associations.
3. Identify lead variants and evaluate independent genetic signals.
4. Prioritise metabolic traits and associated genetic loci.
5. Annotate prioritised genetic variants and investigate their biological functions.
6. Explore overlaps between metabolite-associated loci and published colorectal cancer GWAS findings.

## Dataset

The analysis uses precomputed mGWAS summary statistics generated using REGENIE and provided as part of the MSc research project.

The supplied summary statistics were pre-filtered at P ≤ 1 × 10⁻³ before downstream analysis.

The dataset analysed in this project comprised:

- **44 metabolic traits**
- **6 biochemical groups**
- **408,150 SNP–metabolite association records after quality control**

The six biochemical groups were:

| Amino acids | Circulating amino-acid traits |
| Fatty acids | Fatty-acid metabolic traits |
| Glycolysis | Glycolysis-related metabolites |
| Ketone bodies | Ketone-body traits |
| Lipoprotein concentrations | Lipoprotein concentration measurements |
| Triglycerides | Triglyceride-related measurements |

**Data availability:** The original mGWAS summary statistics are not included in this repository. Access to the underlying datasets is subject to the relevant data-sharing permissions.
